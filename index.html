Interactive demos

  1. Lecture 1 — Why Location MattersLayer Explorer: toggle spatial layers over eastern Christchurch and see how patterns emerge only when layers are combined.
  2. Lecture 2 — Data Quality & EthicsQuality Impact Explorer: how positional error, completeness and currency change an engineering decision.
  3. Lecture 3 — Spatial Data ModelsVector vs Raster: the same landscape represented two ways, and what each model does well.
  4. Lecture 4 — CRS & ProjectionsWhy Projections Matter: distances and areas in WGS84 vs NZTM2000, and what goes wrong when CRS is mismatched.
  5. Lecture 5 — Spatial RelationshipsSpatial Query Explorer: intersects, within, touches and friends, tested live on real features.
  6. Lecture 6 — Vector AnalysisBuller River Catchment Workflow: buffer, spatial join, dissolve and clip step by step.
  7. Lecture 7 — Cartography EssentialsMap Styling Lab: classification methods, colour schemes and the choropleth trap.
  8. Lecture 8 — Raster AnalysisDEM Explorer: slope, hillshade and map algebra on Banks Peninsula.
  9. Lecture 9 — Hydrological AnalysisBuller River Catchment Explorer: flow direction, flow accumulation and stream extraction.
  10. Lecture 10 — Risk Assessment & MCERisk Calculator: hazard × exposure × vulnerability, with adjustable multi-criteria weights.
  11. Lecture 11 — The Future of GISFlood Exposure Map built live with an AI coding agent.
These demos run entirely in the browser. Basemap tiles come from OpenStreetMap and Esri; everything else (data and the Leaflet library) is bundled here, so the demos also work offline if you download this repository.
