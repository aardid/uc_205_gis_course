Guided Questions

Q1 — Accumulation

Switch to Flow accumulation. Hover over a ridge crest, then a valley floor. What values do you get? Now look at Flow direction in the inspector: which neighbour does D8 pick, and why that one?

Q2 — Threshold

Turn on the OpenStreetMap streams. Find the threshold where the extracted network best matches them. Write down the threshold and the network length. Where does the DEM put a stream that the map doesn't have, and which one is wrong?

Q3 — Sinks

Untick Fill sinks and note the number of sinks. Now switch the precision to 0.1 m, then 1 m. It is the same terrain, so where did the extra sinks come from? Place a pour point near the coast on the largest stream and compare its area with filling on and off. Are most sinks real depressions or DEM artefacts?

Q4 — Pour points

Drop a pour point on a stream. Then untick Snap and click one or two cells beside the channel. Why does the catchment collapse? How does this explain a delineated catchment boundary that runs straight through a gully?

Q5 — Culvert design

Imagine a coastal road crossing the main stream. Delineate its catchment and read the rational-method peak flow. Move the culvert 500 m upstream: how do the area and Q change? Which input (C, i or A) would you trust least, and why?
